Hi! Nice to meet you!

Short version: I like soils, environmental sciences and computers.


Since October 2025, I am a PhD student in the Geosciences Department of Ecole normale supérieure (ENS-PSL) in Paris, France, with the supervision of Bertrand GUENET and Samuel ABIVEN. My topic is soil organic matter and its decomposition scheme in the ORCHIDEE-IPSL model. Part of my focus is on pyrogenic carbon in soils and how it affects the carbon dynamics.

Over the last years, I have studied soils through modeling over many different topics: soil pollution, impact of agricultural practices, prediction of carbon stocks, soil metabolic networks and microbes, and finally, pyrogenic carbon.

I am quite fond of what other disciplines can bring to biogeochemistry, from history to art by way of sociology, so I'm always reading, studying, creating to keep deepening my understanding of the world!
