Hello!

Short version: I like soils, environmental sciences and models.

Since October 2025, I am a PhD student in the Geosciences Department of Ecole normale supérieure (ENS-PSL) in Paris, France, under the supervision of Bertrand GUENET and Samuel ABIVEN. I now study soil organic matter and its decomposition scheme in the ORCHIDEE-IPSL model. Part of my focus is on pyrogenic carbon in soils and how it affects the carbon dynamics.

Over the last years, I have studied soils through modeling over many different topics: soil pollution, impact of agricultural practices, prediction of carbon stocks, soil metabolic networks and finally, pyrogenic carbon.

I am quite fond of what other disciplines can bring to biogeochemistry, from history to art by way of sociology, so I always keep an open scientific mind!
