The Dead Geologists Society has been founded by Emile DENISE, Mathieu SORET and me. Every month in the Geosciences departement, we broadcast a movie more or less related to Geosciences, and share bingo grids and pop corn around a collaborative apéro. Sometimes we go out to the movies too.

What is better than complaining about bad scientific representation if not doing it with fellow geoscientists?
