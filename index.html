Hi! Nice to meet you!

Short version: my name is Julie-Maï PARIS (she/her) and I like soils, environmental sciences, art and modeling.


Since October 2025, I am a PhD student in the Geosciences Department of Ecole normale supérieure (ENS-PSL) in Paris, France, with the supervision of Bertrand GUENET and Samuel ABIVEN. My topic is soil organic matter and its decomposition scheme in the ORCHIDEE-IPSL model. Part of my focus is pyrogenic carbon in soils and how its presence affects the carbon dynamics.

Over the last years, I have studied soils through modeling over many different topics: soil pollution, agricultural practices, carbon stability, soil metabolic networks and microbes, and finally, pyrogenic carbon.

To study biogeochemistry, we need other fields too; from history to art by way of sociology. I'm always reading, studying, writing and drawing to enrich my science with a multidisciplinary point of view!
